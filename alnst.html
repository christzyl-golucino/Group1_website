OVERVIEW

Summary of the Plot

Set in a dystopian future where Earth has been invaded and conquered by aliens, humanity has been reduced to pets and entertainment for extra-terrestrial rulers. The ultimate manifestation of this hierarchy is Alien Stage, a brutal vocal competition held inside the elite Anakt Garden complex.

Human contestants raised in captivity are forced into 1v1 singing duels where alien audiences vote on performance, skill, and raw emotion. The winner advances to the next round, while the loser faces immediate execution. As the tournament progresses, deep childhood bonds, unrequited love, and quiet defiance turn every performance into a battlefield of emotional rebellion.
