OVERVIEW

Summary of the Plot

Set in a dystopian future where Earth has been invaded and conquered by aliens, humanity has been reduced to pets and entertainment for extra-terrestrial rulers. The ultimate manifestation of this hierarchy is Alien Stage, a brutal vocal competition held inside the elite Anakt Garden complex.

Human contestants raised in captivity are forced into 1v1 singing duels where alien audiences vote on performance, skill, and raw emotion. The winner advances to the next round, while the loser faces immediate execution. As the tournament progresses, deep childhood bonds, unrequited love, and quiet defiance turn every performance into a battlefield of emotional rebellion.

PERFORMANCES & STAGES

Official Episodes

Explore the official song rounds and special episodes that make up the Alien Stage competition timeline.

🌸 ROUND 1

My Clematis

Mizi vs. Sua

This round introduces the tragic rules of the tournament. Childhood best friends (and lovers) Mizi and Sua are pitted against each other. They sing a deeply emotional, harmonious duet that flashes back to their beautiful days growing up together in Anakt Garden. Mizi wins by a single point (87 to 86), only to watch in horror as Sua is immediately executed for losing.

🎸 ROUND 2

Unknown

Till vs. Acorn

The rebellious, grey-haired rock contestant Till faces off against an opponent named Acorn. Till breaks tournament rules by playing a chaotic punk-rock song he wrote himself about his unrequited love for Mizi. He smashes his guitar and completely dominates the stage, defeating Acorn 153 to 20.

🖤 ROUND 3

Round 3 | Black Sorrow

Ivan vs. Marty

Ivan, a heavily favored human raised by wealthy alien owners, faces off against an opponent named Marty. Ivan delivers a dramatic, operatic orchestral-rock performance that reveals his dark, obsessive fixation on Till. Ivan easily wins the match with a score of 90 to 68.

🩸 ROUND 4 (Off-Screen)

No Official Track Released

Luka vs. Durian

This round takes place entirely off-screen but is briefly depicted via flashbacks. The cold-blooded, elite champion Luka cleanly defeats his unnamed opponent, Durian, leaving him dead on a blood-stained stage and cementing Luka as the season's top favorite.

👑 ROUND 5

Ruler of my Heart

Mizi vs. Luka

Luka and a heavily traumatized Mizi. Luka maliciously mimics Sua's voice and gestures to emotionally break Mizi on stage. Driven to a blind rage, Mizi stops singing entirely to physically assault Luka. Before alien guards can execute her, the human resistance crashes the venue and rescues her.

💔 ROUND 6

Cure

Ivan vs. Till

Forced into a direct duel, childhood friends Ivan and Till face each other. Till, believing Mizi is dead, sings with a completely defeated, melancholic tone. Ivan performs a beautiful track about his unrequited love for Till. Realizing Till has given up and will be executed if he loses, Ivan drops his microphone, forces a kiss on Till, and strangles him. This intentionally triggers the stage's automated safety guards, who shoot and kill Ivan for violence, sacrificing himself so Till survives.

💥 Interlude: The Resistance

All-In

Hyuna & Resistance

Moving away from the official stage brackets, this high-energy rock anthem follows Hyuna and the anti-Alien Stage human rebellion. It tracks Mizi recovering from her trauma and joining the rebel forces as they mount a heavy tactical assault on Anakt Garden to save remaining captives.

🏁 FINALE

Blink Gone

Till vs. Luka

The grand finale of the 50th season between Till and Luka. Weaponizing Till's trauma, Luka mimics the imagery of Ivan choking him to psychologically torture Till mid-performance. The pressure drives Till into a severe panic attack and deep hallucinations, causing him to break down and stop singing entirely. Luka wins the tournament, while Till suffers a brutal physical and mental collapse.

🕊️ Epilogue Backstory

Wiege

Luka & Hyuna Memory

Rather than a competitive duel, this haunting orchestral cinematic features Luka and Hyuna reminiscing. It explores their intertwined childhood history and past relationship long before the current tournament, shedding light on the tragic motivations that drive Luka's compliance and Hyuna's rebellion.

🪐 Season Finale

Karma

Sua & Mizi

The ultimate climax of the main storyline. Following the aftermath of the final round, Mizi confronts Luka on the collapsing stage. Drowning in immense survivor's guilt over losing Sua, Hyuna, and Till, Mizi violently attacks Luka and denounces the cycle of exploitation. She then shoots the alien host Isaac, takes control of his tablet, and forces a missile to target the stadium in a nihilistic bid to destroy the entire system once and for all
