Step onto the Stage

Summary of the Plot

"May this song be my last rebellion."

Set in a dystopian future where Earth has been invaded and conquered by aliens, humanity has been reduced to pets and entertainment for the extra-terrestrial rulers. The ultimate manifestation of this system is Alien Stage, a brutal reality vocal tournament held inside the elite Anakt Garden complex.

Human contestants, raised in captivity, are forced to face each other in 1v1 singing duels where audiences vote on performance and emotion. The winner advances to the next round, while the loser faces immediate elimination—execution. As the competition progresses, deep childhood bonds, unspoken love, and quiet acts of defiance turn the gruesome stage into a battlefield of emotional rebellion.

Main Contestants

The human performers competing in the tournament

Mizi

Contestant #1

A bright, energetic, and empathetic girl with pink hair whose vibrant singing style hides deep emotional turbulence. She shares a profound bond with Sua.

Sua

Contestant #2

A quiet, composed singer with a soft, melancholic voice. Her devotion to protecting Mizi drives her every choice on the stage.

Till

Contestant #3

A rebellious rock artist known for his gritty vocals and fierce resistance against alien authority. He holds long-standing, unrequited feelings for Mizi.

Ivan

Contestant #4

A calculating and charismatic singer with dark motives and a tragic past. His intense obsession with Till leads to dramatic confrontations.

Luka

Champion

The top performer of Anakt Garden. Elegant, cold, and immensely skilled, he dominates the tournament with unyielding perfection.

Hyuna

Rebel / Survivor

A former contestant who escaped the alien system. She leads human resistance forces operating outside Anakt Garden's walls.
