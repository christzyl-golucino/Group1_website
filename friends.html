Meet The Gang

#01 • Fashion Icon

Rachel Green

Portrayed by Jennifer Aniston
Ambitious Stylish Independent

From runaway bride cutting up credit cards to executive at Ralph Lauren, Rachel's journey is an inspiring story of self-discovery and independence.

"Ah, humor based on my pain. Ah, good one."
#02 • Master Chef

Monica Geller

Portrayed by Courteney Cox
Competitive Organized Nurturing

The glue that holds the group together. Monica is an exceptionally talented chef whose passion for perfection, cleanliness, and hosting keeps everyone fed and together.

"I KNOW!"
#03 • Free Spirit

Phoebe Buffay

Portrayed by Lisa Kudrow
Eccentric Musician Loyal

A quirkily optimistic musician and masseuse with a colorful past. Singer of "Smelly Cat", Phoebe brings uninhibited joy and eccentric logic to the gang.

"They don't know that we know they know we know."
#04 • Actor

Joey Tribbiani

Portrayed by Matt LeBlanc
Charismatic Foodie Big Heart

A good-natured actor best known for playing Dr. Drake Ramoray on Days of Our Lives. Joey loves food, women, and his friends above all else.

"How you doin'?"
#05 • Sarcasm King

Chandler Bing

Portrayed by Matthew Perry
Sarcastic Witty Devoted

Master of defense mechanisms and quick wit. Chandler navigates career re-invention and commitment fears to become the ultimate loving partner to Monica.

"Could this BE any more iconic?"
#06 • Paleontologist

Ross Geller

Portrayed by David Schwimmer
Intellectual Hopeless Romantic Dinosaur Fan

Monica's older brother and a passionate paleontologist. Ross's turbulent romance with Rachel forms the emotional core of the series' overarching story.

"WE WERE ON A BREAK!"
