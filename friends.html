THE STORY

Summary of the Plot

"I'll be there for you..."

F.R.I.E.N.D.S follows the lives of six close friends living in New York City: Rachel Green, Monica Geller, Phoebe Buffay, Joey Tribbiani, Chandler Bing, and Ross Geller.

Throughout the series, the group experiences the ups and downs of adulthood, including relationships, careers, family, friendship, and everyday life.

Whether they are spending time at Central Perk, hanging out at Monica's apartment, celebrating important milestones, or getting themselves into ridiculous situations, the six friends remain at the center of one another's lives.

THE GANG

Meet the Friends

Six completely different personalities. One unforgettable friendship.

01

Rachel Green

Fashion • Independent • Ambitious

Rachel begins a new independent life in New York and gradually builds a successful career while navigating friendship, love, and adulthood.

02

Monica Geller

Chef • Competitive • Organized

Monica is Ross's younger sister and the group's famously organized chef. Her apartment becomes one of the main gathering places for the friends.

03

Phoebe Buffay

Musician • Free-Spirited • Unique

Phoebe is the eccentric musician of the group. Her unusual perspective and unpredictable personality make her one of the most memorable members of the gang.

04

Joey Tribbiani

Actor • Food Lover • Loyal

Joey is an aspiring actor with a big heart, an enormous appetite, and a memorable sense of humor. His friendship with Chandler is one of the show's defining relationships.

05

Chandler Bing

Sarcastic • Witty • Caring

Chandler is known for his sarcastic humor and quick one-liners. Beneath the jokes, he is a caring friend who values the people closest to him.

06

Ross Geller

Paleontologist • Intelligent • Awkward

Ross is Monica's older brother and a passionate paleontologist. His relationships, interests, and personality provide many memorable moments throughout the series.
