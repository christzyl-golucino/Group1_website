THE WORLD OF NEN

SUMMARY OF THE PLOT

The story follows Gon Freecss, a young boy who discovers that his father Ging—who he believed to be dead—is alive and renowned as one of the greatest Hunters in the world. Hunters are elite individuals licensed to track down secret treasures, rare beasts, and dangerous fugitives.

Driven to understand why his father chose his career over him, Gon leaves Whale Island to take the deadly Hunter Examination. Along his journey, he forms unbreakable bonds with Killua, Kurapika, and Leorio as they unlock Nen—the supernatural ability to manipulate one's own life energy.
