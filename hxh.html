Summary of the Plot

"If you want to know the person, find out what makes them angry."

The story focuses on a young boy named Gon Freecss, who discovers that his father—who he was told had passed away—is actually alive and a world-renowned Hunter. Hunters are licensed professionals who specialize in locating rare animal species, treasure hunting, surveying unexplored territory, or tracking down lawless individuals.

Determined to follow in his father's footsteps, Gon leaves his home on Whale Island to take the notoriously difficult Hunter Examination. Along the way, he meets and befriends three fellow applicants: Killua Zoldyck, Kurapika, and Leorio. Together, they embark on dangerous adventures and uncover the power of Nen—a technique that allows a living being to manipulate their own life energy (aura).

Main Characters

The Hunters and their unique Nen categories

Gon Freecss

Enhancer

An athletic, naive, and friendly boy determined to become a great Hunter to understand why his father chose the profession over raising him.

Killua Zoldyck

Transmuter

A prodigy from the infamous Zoldyck family of elite assassins. He befriends Gon during the Hunter Exam to escape his family's deadly legacy.

Kurapika

Conjurer / Specialist

The sole survivor of the Kurta Clan. He seeks to become a Blacklist Hunter to avenge his slaughtered clan and retrieve their stolen Scarlet Eyes.

Leorio Paradinight

Emitter

A hot-headed young man who enters the Hunter Exam to get money to attend medical school and become a doctor who helps the poor for free.

Hisoka Morow

Transmuter

A bizarre, sadistic magician obsessed with finding strong opponents to fight and kill. He takes a twisted interest in Gon and Killua's growth.
